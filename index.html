DO YOU KNOW ABOUT

GENTRIFICATION?

to put it simply: People with a lot of money move to a specific district, where poorer people live. Resulting in an overall enhancement on the appearance of that district. For example, there will probably be more coffee shops and restaurants opening. Eventually, rent prices will increase significantly, making it impossible for the initial residents to pay for rent, which will lead to them having to move out. Rinse, repeat this progress and eventually there will only be rich people left.
